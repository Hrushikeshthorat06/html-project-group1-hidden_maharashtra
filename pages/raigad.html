Hostory of Raigad Fort

Raigad Fort is a famous historical fort located in the Raigad district of Maharashtra, India. It was originally known as Rairi Fort. In 1656, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj captured the fort from Chandrarao More of Jawali. Recognizing its strategic importance, Shivaji Maharaj developed it into a strong and well-planned capital. On 6 June 1674, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was crowned as the king of the Maratha Empire at Raigad Fort. The fort became the political and administrative centre of his kingdom. Raigad Fort is surrounded by steep hills and strong fortifications, making it difficult for enemies to attack. It also contains important historical structures such as the Maha Darwaja, Raj Bhavan, Jagadishwar Temple, and the Samadhi of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

Features of Raigad Fort

1. Strategic Location: Situated on a hill in the Sahyadri mountain range, Raigad Fort offers natural protection.
2. Maha Darwaja: The main entrance of the fort, designed for security and defence.
3. Royal Palace: The remains of the palace show the historical importance of Raigad as the capital of the Maratha Empire.
4. Shivaji Maharaj's Samadhi: The memorial of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj is located inside the fort.
5. Jagadishwar Temple: A historic temple situated within the fort.
6. Takmak Tok: A famous cliff that offers a beautiful view of the surrounding mountains.
7. Water Storage: The fort has several water tanks and reservoirs that helped provide water to its residents.
8. Historical Importance: Raigad Fort was the capital of Chhatrapati Shivaji Mahara"s kingdom and the site of his coronation in 1674.
