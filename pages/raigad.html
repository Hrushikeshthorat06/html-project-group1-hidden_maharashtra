Raigad Fort - The Capital of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Raigad Fort is a famous historical fort located in the Raigad district of Maharashtra, India. It is situated in the Sahyadri mountain range. Raigad Fort was the capital of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's kingdom. Shivaji Maharaj was crowned as the king of the Maratha Empire at Raigad Fort in 1674. The fort is known for its historical importance, beautiful mountain views, and Maratha architecture. It is one of the most important tourist destinations in Maharashtra. Location: Raigad District, Maharashtra Historical Importance: Capital of the Maratha Empire Famous For: Shivaji Maharaj's coronation in 1674 Attractions: Maha Darwaja, Raj Bhavan, Jagadishwar Temple, and Shivaji Maharaj's Samadhi. Raigad Fort represents the bravery, courage, and glorious history of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.
