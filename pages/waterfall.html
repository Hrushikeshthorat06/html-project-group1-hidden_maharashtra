Explore the Hidden Waterfalls of Maharashtra

Discover the magical waterfalls of Maharashtra, surrounded by lush green forests, misty mountains, and peaceful valleys. Explore nature's hidden beauty and find your next adventure.

1. Thoseghar Waterfall

Thoseghar Waterfall is a beautiful waterfall located near Satara, Maharashtra. It is surrounded by green hills, forests, and peaceful natural scenery.

Best known for its scenic views during the monsoon, Thoseghar is a wonderful destination for nature lovers and photography enthusiasts.

Bhambavli Vajrai Waterfall

Vajrai Waterfall is located near Bhambavli village in Satara district. It is famous for its impressive height and stunning natural surroundings.

The waterfall looks especially beautiful in the rainy season, with lush greenery and misty hills creating a magical atmosphere.

Devkund Waterfall

Devkund Waterfall is a scenic waterfall near Bhira village in Raigad district, Maharashtra. It is admired for its beautiful water and forest surroundings.

The journey to Devkund is popular among adventure lovers. Visit only when access is officially open and follow local safety instructions.

Kalu Waterfall

Kalu Waterfall is a spectacular waterfall in the Malshej Ghat region of Maharashtra. It is surrounded by dramatic cliffs, green valleys, and beautiful hills.

During the monsoon, the waterfall creates a breathtaking natural view. It is a lovely place for nature lovers who enjoy exploring Maharashtra.
