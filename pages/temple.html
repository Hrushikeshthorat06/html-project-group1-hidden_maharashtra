1. Kedareshwar Cave Temple (Paachnai)

Kedareshwar Cave Temple is located near Harishchandragad in Maharashtra. It is situated inside a natural cave and houses a massive Shiva lingam surrounded by cold water.

According to local legend, four pillars once supported the cave, but three have broken. The legend says that the world will end when the final pillar falls. The cave is known for its mysterious atmosphere and natural beauty.

2. Bhuleshwar Temple (Malshiras)

Bhuleshwar Temple is an ancient hilltop temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, located near Pune, Maharashtra. It is known for its beautiful stone carvings and distinctive architectural style.

The temple is believed to date back to the 13th century. Its architecture combines distinctive decorative elements, and it is associated with the rare depiction of Ganeshwari, a female form of Lord Ganesha.

3. Shri Kopeshwar Temple (Khidrapur)

Shri Kopeshwar Temple is located in Khidrapur, Kolhapur district, Maharashtra, on the banks of the Krishna River. It is admired for its remarkable stone architecture and historical importance.

The temple is famous for its open-roof Swargamandapa, also known as the heavenly hall. Its walls and pillars feature detailed carvings of elephants, deities, and other decorative figures.

4. Nageshwar Cave Temple (Met Indawli)

Nageshwar Cave Temple is described as a hidden Shiva temple in a cliffside cave near Met Indawli in the Satara region of Maharashtra.

Surrounded by forest and scenic landscapes, the destination is associated with a trek through natural trails. Visitors should check local access conditions and follow safety instructions before planning a visit.
