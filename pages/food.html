Discover the delicious flavours of Maharashtra, from spicy street food and traditional meals to sweet delicacies. Explore the state's rich culinary heritage and enjoy the unique tastes of different regions.

1. Misal Pav

Misal Pav is a popular Maharashtrian dish made with spicy sprouted moth beans curry, known as misal, and served with soft bread called pav.

It is usually topped with farsan, chopped onions, coriander, and lemon juice. Misal Pav is especially popular in Nashik, Pune, and Kolhapur.

2. Vada Pav

Vada Pav is one of Maharashtra's most famous street foods. It consists of a spicy potato fritter placed inside a pav.

It is often served with green chutney, dry garlic chutney, and fried green chillies. This delicious snack is especially associated with Mumbai.

3. Puran Poli

Puran Poli is a traditional Maharashtrian sweet flatbread prepared with a filling of cooked chana dal, jaggery, and aromatic spices.

It is commonly prepared during festivals such as Holi and Gudi Padwa. Puran Poli is often served with ghee and is loved for its sweet flavour.

4. Thalipeeth

Thalipeeth, a traditional multi-grain flatbread, is a culinary gem of Mumbai, Maharashtra, celebrated for its rich flavors and nutritional value. This dish not only represents the vibrant culture of the city but also showcases the region's unique agricultural heritage.

Thalipeeth is more than just a dish; it embodies the spirit of Mumbai, where bustling breakfast stalls offer this wholesome delicacy to countless commuters. Traditionally prepared using a variety of flours and spices, it serves as a symbol of local identity, reflecting the diverse communities that call Mumbai home.
